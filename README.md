# KrushiSetu 🌾

KrushiSetu is a smart digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with verified buyers and helps farmers make better crop-selling decisions.

## Problem

Farmers often face difficulties in finding the right buyers, getting transparent market prices, comparing different markets, and understanding the actual return after transportation and other costs. Dependence on intermediaries can also reduce their earnings.

## Solution

KrushiSetu provides a single digital platform where farmers can list their crops, access market information, connect with verified buyers, compare selling opportunities, and make smarter decisions to achieve fairer returns.

## Key Features

- Farmer, Buyer and Admin portals
- Direct farmer-to-buyer connection
- Real-time market price information
- AI-based price and demand prediction
- Verified buyer matching
- Market comparison
- Crop quality information
- Logistics and delivery tracking
- Secure digital payments
- Admin verification and monitoring
- Multilingual support: English, Gujarati, Hindi and Marathi

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript / React
- Backend: Node.js
- Database: Supabase
- AI/ML: Market price and demand prediction
- GIS: Location-based market and logistics support
- Payment Integration: Secure digital payment gateway

## Expected Impact

KrushiSetu aims to improve market access for farmers, reduce dependence on unnecessary intermediaries, increase price transparency, reduce selling and logistics costs, and help farmers receive fairer returns for their produce.

## Target Users

- Farmers
- Buyers
- Agricultural markets / Mandis
- Logistics providers
- Platform administrators

## Project Vision

To create a transparent and efficient digital agricultural marketplace where every farmer can connect with the right buyer and make informed decisions about where and when to sell their crops.
